Fake credit alerts, “BVN update” links, job offers with a training fee. Paste any message and Scam Shield tells you if it is a scam. Gemini Nano reads it on your own device. When the cloud is needed, it gets a redacted copy, and only after you say yes.

How it works

Your message never leaves your device unredacted.

  1. 01

    Read on your device

    If Chrome has Gemini Nano, the check runs locally with Firebase AI Logic in ONLY_ON_DEVICE mode. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  2. 02

    Redact before the cloud

    No local model, or a very long message? Phone and account numbers, BVN, OTPs, cards, emails, link paths and names are removed on your device first. You see the exact text, then choose.

  3. 03

    Warn others

    Share the redacted copy and see how many people reported the same message this week. Explanations come in Hausa, Yorùbá, Igbo and Pidgin.

Built on Firebase AI Logic hybrid inference, App Check, Remote Config, Authentication and Firestore. Open source under Apache-2.0. Get the Chrome extension from the GitHub repository.
